Security Guidelines

Section 1: Password Policy

All employees must use passwords that are at least twelve characters long
and include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols.
Passwords must be changed every 90 days. Password reuse across the last
five passwords is not allowed.



Section 2: Device Security

All company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled before they
leave the office. Screens must be locked automatically after five minutes
of inactivity. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the security
team within one hour of discovery.



Section 3: Incident Reporting

Any suspected security incident must be reported to the security team
through the internal ticketing system within twenty-four hours. Employees
must never attempt to investigate a suspected breach on their own; all
evidence must be preserved for the security team.
